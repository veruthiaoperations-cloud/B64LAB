# Contributing & Governance Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in **B64Lab**!

---

## ️ Project Governance & Maintenance

** B64Lab** is an authoritative reference implementation and educational workbench designed, maintained, and curated directly by **Veruthia Consulting LLC**.

To preserve the educational curriculum integrity, architectural consistency, and zero-dependency design philosophy of this project:

*** External Pull Requests (PRs):** We are currently **not accepting unsolicited external pull requests**. The core architecture and challenge modules are maintained exclusively by Veruthia Consulting LLC.
*** Forking & Modifying:** You are completely free (and encouraged!) to **fork this repository**, modify the code, build your own challenges, and adapt it for your team's internal training programs in accordance with the [MIT License](LICENSE).
*** Feedback & Bug Reports:** If you discover a bug or error in the educational material, please open a standard GitHub Issue. We review all feedback regularly.
